# Tasks

Group 1 comes first; group 2 does not depend on it. The manual check of
group 3 needs both. Each task names the test that shows it works.

## 1. Spring

- [x] 1.1 Add the spring to `ListState` in `virtual_list.rs`: the fields `pending` and `velocity`, a function that adds wheel input and drops what reaches past either end, and the step of decision 1 with `STIFFNESS` 8, which covers at most 0.1 s, rests below 0.5 points and 5 points per second, and stops at either end and at its target; let `scroll_to`, `scroll_by`, `apply` and `reveal` end the motion, `reveal` also when the row is already in view (design, decisions 1 and 3); verify unit tests that one notch of 40 points rests within 1 s at 60 frames per second and never passes its target, that the position after 0.5 s is the same at 30 and at 60 frames per second, that a step of 2 s moves the list as far as a step of 0.1 s, that a burst of 681 points changes the distance per frame by less than 2 % of it, that a second burst during a motion keeps the velocity, that scrolling back by 150 points 0.3 s after a burst of 681 points stops the list at its target without passing it, that `scroll_to`, `scroll_by`, `apply` and `reveal` end the motion, that neither end makes the list move back, that rows added or removed during a motion keep the list within them, and that the existing unit tests of `virtual_list.rs` pass
- [x] 1.2 Sort the wheel events of a pass as decision 2 describes into points to move at once and points for the spring, and keep in egui's temporary data whether a touchpad gesture is under way and the points the lists took that egui has not passed on yet (`owed`); let `ui::show` read this wheel state at the start of every pass and take up to `owed` from `smooth_scroll_delta.y`; verify unit tests for each unit (`Point`, `Line` with `line_scroll_speed`, `Page` with the height of the list), for the zoom modifier and the horizontal modifier, for a gesture from `Start` to `End` across passes, for `Point` steps below 8 points and of 8 points or more, and for `owed`: input a list took is taken from `smooth_scroll_delta.y` in later passes up to its amount and only in its direction, `owed` never goes past 0, and the end of a gesture and a rest below one point clear it
- [x] 1.3 Let `VirtualList::show` take this input while its response is hovered, add what it took to `owed`, set egui's `smooth_scroll_delta.y` to 0, step the spring with `stable_dt` in every pass in which the list is drawn, hovered or not, end a motion at its target when the list was not drawn in the previous pass, and ask for another frame while it moves (design, decisions 1 and 2); verify UI tests in `tests/virtual_list.rs` with a harness that steps at 60 frames per second for the scenarios "Bursts of a touchpad", "One notch of the mouse wheel", "Touchpad gesture", "A key ends the motion", "End of the list" and "Turned back during a motion" of `application-shell`, and a UI test there that Ctrl and the wheel do not scroll the list; UI tests of the window for the scenario "Pointer leaves the list" in `diff_view.rs`, for the commit list not moving when the wheel turns over it while the settings dialog is open in `settings_dialog.rs` (requirement "Settings dialog" of `app-settings`), and for a tab left during a motion showing its commit list at rest at the target when the user comes back in `shortcuts.rs` (scenario "Switching tabs keeps state" of `application-shell`); and that the existing tests of `tests/virtual_list.rs` and `tests/benchmarks.rs` that turn the wheel pass
- [ ] 1.4 Add a pass with the mouse wheel alone to the benchmark `scrolling` in `tests/benchmarks.rs`, after the history has loaded, so that the frames in which the spring moves are measured, and describe it in `docs/benchmarks.md` (design, decision 5); verify that `cargo test --release -p gitbull-app --test benchmarks scrolling -- --ignored --nocapture` passes on Windows against the target "Frame time while scrolling, during and after loading" of `commit-history`, and that its rows, the machine and the date are recorded in `docs/benchmarks.md`
