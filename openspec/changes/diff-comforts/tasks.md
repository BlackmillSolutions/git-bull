# Tasks

Group 1 builds the diff document and the background work in the core,
which every later group draws from. Groups 2 to 5 each bring one comfort
into the diff, with the tests of its scenarios; group 6 measures that the
diff stays fluid, group 7 holds what the user checks, and group 8 the final
check. Each task names the test that shows it works, and each test of new
behaviour fails before its code.

## 1. The diff document

- [x] 1.1 Let `DiffLine` remember with `crlf` whether its line ended in a carriage return and a line feed, where `parse_diff` drops the `\r` (design, decision 5); verify unit tests in `crates/gitbull-git/src/diff.rs` that a CRLF line keeps `crlf`, an LF line does not, and two diffs that differ only in their line endings are not equal
- [x] 1.2 Add `gitbull_core::diff_document` with `DiffDocument`, built from a `FileDiff` and the lines of the new version: its rows with headers, lines, gaps and revealed lines, `gaps()` with their counts and offers, `expand(gap, Part)` by 20 lines at the top or the bottom or all of a gap of at most 20, the old and new numbers of revealed lines, `hunk(row)`, keys of headers, lines and revealed lines that survive revealing, and `widest_number()`; prepare the rows, the row of every key, the rows of the headers and the widest number when the document is built, never when it is only read (design, decisions 1 and 8); verify unit tests of gaps before, between and after hunks, of revealing at the top, at the bottom and all, of the numbers of revealed lines, of no gaps for added, deleted, binary and submodule files, of counts without offers when the text is missing, of no gap after a truncated diff, of keys that find their line after revealing, of no key and no hunk for a gap and no hunk for a revealed line, and of a `widest_number()` that covers the lines after the last hunk and stays the same when they are revealed
- [x] 1.3 Add `next_hunk(row)` and `previous_hunk(row)` to `DiffDocument` as binary searches over the rows of the headers (design, decisions 1 and 7); verify unit tests from a row before, inside and after hunks, and with revealed lines between them
- [x] 1.4 Add the dependency `similar` and compute the changed words of `DiffDocument`: pairs within a block of removed and added lines, the split into words as byte ranges, the threshold of half the longer line counted without whitespace, the byte ranges of words not in common with whitespace included, a changed line ending for pairs of equal text, no marks above 1,000 characters, a deadline of 5 ms per pair, and marks stored by the index of their line (design, decision 3); verify unit tests for each of these, among them the scenarios "One word changed", "Lines with little in common", "Indented lines with little in common", "Change of whitespace" and "Line without a partner" of `diff-view`, and that `cargo deny check` accepts the dependency
- [x] 1.5 Split the background work of `DiffPane` into the three steps of decision 2: changed words from the diff alone, the versions read each on its own and kept as one string with the starts of its lines, and the highlighting from the versions kept; find the line ending of revealed lines in the working copy as Git compares it; let the pane own the document, rebuild its rows when a result arrives, keep every result and what is revealed across a refresh that reads the same diff without starting a worker, drop them for another file or other content, highlight again on a change of theme without reading again, and add `expand` to the session for each pane like `load_whole` (design, decisions 1, 2 and 5); verify unit tests of the pane that the words arrive without waiting for the versions, that the text arrives with the versions and the colours after them, that an old version over 512 KiB leaves the text of the new one but no colours, that a new version over the limit leaves words but no text, that the whole diff after `load_whole` gets its words and keeps its versions and colours without reading again, that a refresh of the same diff keeps words, text, colours and what is revealed and starts no worker, that a change of theme does not read the versions again, that another file forgets what is revealed, and with a repository with `core.autocrlf=true` and a CRLF file in the working copy that revealed lines have no `\r` and end in LF
